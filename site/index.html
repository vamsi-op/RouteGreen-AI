The Problem

Why Traditional Logistics Falls Short

Transport accounts for ~16% of global greenhouse gas emissions. Traditional fleet routing optimizes for distance alone, ignoring the physics of fuel consumption.

📦

Inefficient Packing

Manual cargo loading achieves only ~45% volume utilisation, wasting truck capacity and increasing the number of trips required.

🧭

Distance-Only Routing

GPS routing minimizes distance but ignores elevation and cargo weight — pulling heavy loads uphill burns 20–30% more fuel.

📊

No Emission Auditing

Most operations lack segment-level CO₂ tracking needed for Scope 3 reporting under GRI 302/305 and CSRD standards.

“How might we use AI to optimize cargo load packing and delivery route sequencing so that transport-related fuel usage and Scope 3 carbon emissions can be minimized?”
How It Works

Three-Stage Optimization Pipeline

From cargo loading to route sequencing to ESG compliance — each stage feeds into the next.

01

3D Bin Packing

Extreme-Point First-Fit-Decreasing

Items sorted by volume are packed using 6 axis-aligned orientations, minimizing wasted space while respecting weight limits and keeping the centre of mass low.

81.3% volume efficiency
02

Carbon-Aware Routing

Genetic Algorithm VRP

A GA evolves delivery sequences using a slope-sensitive, load-dependent fuel model. Cargo drops at each stop, so delivery order changes total fuel burned.

7.4% fuel reduction
03

ESG Compliance Audit

IBM Granite Multi-Agent QA

An Eco-Auditor validates load limits and emission ledgers; an Executive Reporting Agent drafts audit-ready ESG summaries aligned with GRI 302/305.

4 audit checks passed

Responsible AI

Built with Ethics in Mind

Every project must address fairness, transparency, ethics and privacy. Here is how RouteGreen AI meets these principles.

⚖️

Fairness

Route scoring relies exclusively on physical variables — distance, road grade and cargo weight. No demographic or biased variables are used.

🔍

Transparency

The pipeline is fully deterministic and auditable. Every route decision is traceable through the segment-by-segment audit trail.

🛡️

Ethics

The system exists solely for environmental benefit — reducing fuel waste and carbon emissions — with no harmful or misleading outputs.

🔒

Privacy

All coordinates and packages in the prototype are simulated and anonymous. No personal data is collected, stored or processed.
